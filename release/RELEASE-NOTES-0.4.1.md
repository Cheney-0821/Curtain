# Curtain 0.4.1

## 简体中文

- **局部清晰开关。** 悬停在已绘制区域上即可暂时模糊或恢复清晰。开合带有渐变，重叠区域会自动合并。
- **窗口保护菜单。** 右键窗口的主开关即可打开保护菜单，开启点击穿透时也能编辑清晰区域。
- **应用兼容性。** 改善微信和企业微信表情面板的遮罩表现，并修复关闭「使用全局设置」后部分选项仍不可编辑的问题。

## English

- **Clear area controls.** Hover over a drawn area to blur or reveal it with a smooth transition. Overlapping areas merge automatically.
- **Window protection menu.** Right-click the main window control to open the protection menu and edit clear areas while click-through is enabled.
- **App compatibility.** Improved blur coverage for emoji panels in WeChat and WeCom, and fixed per-app controls remaining disabled after switching away from global settings.
