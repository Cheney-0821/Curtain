# Curtain 0.4.2

## 简体中文

- **移动局部清晰区域。** 按住区域开关即可拖动。清晰范围实时跟随，移开的部分恢复模糊。
- **调整区域大小。** 区域处于模糊状态时，拖动蓝色边框或角落即可缩放。移动和缩放均限制在窗口内。

## English

- **Move clear areas.** Drag an area's toggle to move it. The clear area follows as you drag, and blur returns where the area moves away.
- **Resize areas.** Drag the blue border or a corner while an area is blurred. Moving and resizing stay within the window.
