# Curtain 0.4.4

## 简体中文

- **清晰区域自动保存。** 重新打开应用或重启电脑后，可以继续使用已绘制的区域。改善了区域开关在窗口激活后的显示。
- **应用保护集中管理。** 已添加的应用关闭后仍可在原列表管理。移除保护时会一并清除该应用的清晰区域。
- **首次使用。** 窗口保护默认采用中度模糊，点击穿透默认关闭。

## English

- **Saved clear areas.** Clear areas are available again after reopening an app or restarting Windows. Region controls now appear more reliably after interacting with a protected window.
- **App management.** Manage added apps in one list, even while they are closed. Removing an app's protection also removes its saved clear areas.
- **First-run settings.** Window protection starts with medium blur and click-through turned off.
